Notre Histoire

A Parisian patisserie,
in the heart of Pune.

Since 2012, Le Plaisir has brought the unhurried pleasure of a Parisian bistro to Prabhat Road — flaky pastry in the morning, wood-fired pizza by evening, and a patisserie counter worth lingering at.

At its heart is chef-owner Siddharth Mahadik, trained at the Cordon Bleu — a kitchen where French technique meets Pune's appetite, served in warm, vibrant yellow rooms that feel like sunshine even on a grey day.
