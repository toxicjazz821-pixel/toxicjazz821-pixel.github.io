Welcome to Aurelia

A family palace, run like a promise since 1962.

The Aurelia began as the lakeside residence of the Sisodia household and opened its doors to travellers in 1962. Three generations on, the marble is the same, the recipes are the same, and the welcome — we are told — has only grown warmer.

Days here move at lake speed: breakfast on the terrace, a boat at golden hour, dinner under a ceiling of stars and brass lamps.
